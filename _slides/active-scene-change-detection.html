WHAT IS NBVS?

Knowing what to see next

  • A minimal subset of key images can match the PSNR of the entire dataset.
  • The next informative view is predicted from camera pose—without pixel information.

Wilson et al. “POp-GS: Next Best View in 3D-Gaussian Splatting with P-Optimality.” arXiv:2412.03911, 2024.
